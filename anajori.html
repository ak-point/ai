Who We Are

Creating Opportunities for a Better Society

Anajori Development Society is a non-profit organisation established with a commitment to social welfare and sustainable community development.

Since its establishment in 2015, the organisation has been working to improve the quality of life of people through community-based initiatives in health, education, agriculture, livelihoods and women empowerment.

  • Community health and awareness
  • Education and digital learning
  • Women empowerment and livelihood development
  • Sustainable agriculture and animal health
