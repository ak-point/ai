Know About Us

Working for Healthier and Stronger Communities

Anajori Development Society is a non-governmental organisation formed to support underprivileged communities and create awareness among rural people.

The Society works in human and animal health, awareness programmes and agriculture. Its activities include free veterinary vaccination camps, veterinary extension awareness, animal health awareness, scientific poultry and pig farming, zoonotic disease prevention, wildlife conservation and scientific dairy farming.

The organisation was formed on 16 September 2015 following a general meeting held at Kalafakirtola LP School and was registered on 13 November 2015 under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.
