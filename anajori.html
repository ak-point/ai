Who We Are

Building Bridges to Opportunity at the Grassroots

Founded with a deep commitment to social equity and holistic rural upliftment, Anajori Development Society partners directly with marginalized families across Assam and Northeast India.

Rather than offering temporary charity, we co-create sustainable ecosystems—equipping women, youth, and children with quality education, market-ready skills, and preventive healthcare access.

Community-Led Growth

Initiatives designed alongside village elders and self-help groups to respect local heritage and real needs.

Transparency & Accountability

Strict financial governance and open impact reporting ensure every rupee creates measurable grassroots value.

Inclusive Participation

Prioritizing equal opportunities for rural women, first-generation learners, and underserved communities.

Our Core Focus Areas

Holistic Programs for Lasting Change

Our four interconnected thematic pillars address the root causes of rural vulnerability, empowering individuals from childhood through adulthood.

Education & Child Development

Bridging learning gaps through remedial after-school centers, foundational literacy, digital classrooms, and school supply kits for underprivileged children.

Livelihood & Skill Training

Empowering rural women and youth with traditional handloom weaving, tailoring, financial literacy, and micro-enterprise incubation support.

Health & Community Well-being

Delivering mobile rural health camps, maternal & child nutrition awareness, menstrual hygiene workshops, and clean sanitation drives.

Sustainable Environment & Agriculture

Championing native tree plantation drives, flood-resilient organic farming practices, rainwater harvesting, and climate awareness for farmers.
